21Board · A guide for teachers

A bag of tricks
for every classroom.

21Board turns your smartboard into a board full of classroom tools: timers, notes, a name picker, and more. Open one, use it, move on.

Start in three taps

There’s nothing to install, no account, and no Save button. Every change is saved on the device as you work.

  1. Open 21Board

    Open it from your Apps menu. A new board says “Your board is ready.”

  2. Tap [W]

    The Widget Picker sits in both bottom corners, so you can reach it from either side of the board. Settings is the gear in its bottom row.

  3. Tap a widget

    It opens in its own window. Open as many as you like, and come back to them tomorrow just as you left them.

Meet the widgets

Seventeen tools, each one tap away. Tap a tile to see what it does, with ideas from kindergarten to eighth grade.

Time Timer, Task Timer, Clock, Calendar

{{icon:timer}}

Timer

A countdown with quick presets and the alarm you choose.

Try it

  1. Tap a preset: 1m, 2m, 3m, 5m, 10m, or 15m.
  2. Tap ▶ to start and ⏸ to pause.
  3. At zero the window flashes red and the alarm plays.

Ideas for class

  • K–2Tap 3m for clean-up. The shrinking bar and the red window say time’s up, no reading needed.
  • 3–5Think, pair, share. Two minutes to think, then two to talk.
  • 6–8A 5-minute bell-ringer as students walk in. Choose Gentle for a soft ending.

Good to know

  • Tap +1:00 while it runs for one more minute.
  • Tap Timer ⌄ in the title bar to pick a sound. It plays as you pick it.
  • In the last minute it turns amber. Change that in Timer ⌄.
  • It rings only on the page you’re on.
{{icon:task-timer}}

Task Timer

Your lesson’s parts as a pie that counts down each one.

Try it

  1. Tap the circle, then + Add activity for each part of the lesson.
  2. Type its name and time. Tap its emoji to pick another.
  3. Tap Done, then ▶ Start.
  4. When the alarm rings, tap 🔔 Stop alarm to move on.

Ideas for class

  • K–2A morning routine pre-readers follow by emoji and color: 🎒 Unpack, 👥 Morning meeting, 📖 Read to self.
  • 3–5Four stations, 12 minutes each. The Next line under the pie shows where everyone goes.
  • 6–8Plan a class period with ⏱ Fixed time. A blank slice shows the spare minutes.

Good to know

  • Tap a slice to jump to that activity.
  • Move it by the title bar or with two fingers, since you type in it.
{{icon:clock}}

Clock

An analog or digital clock, or both. Set any time for a lesson.

Try it

  1. Open Clock. It starts as an analog clock.
  2. Tap Clock ⌄ and choose Simple, Old, or Teaching.
  3. Check Digital too. Both show together.
  4. Check Set the time. Drag a hand, or tap ▲ and ▼.
  5. Tap Now for the real time again.

Ideas for class

  • K–2Mark a time for clean-up. Tap the face where the minute hand will be. The shading shrinks until it gets there.
  • 1Teaching with Hour hand only. Read the hour first, then add the minute hand.
  • 1–3Check Digital and Quiz, then set a time. Students read the hands. Tap ? to show the time.
  • 3–5The Old face for Roman numerals. Find IIII, then check IV for four.
  • 6–8Digital with 24-hour time for a science lab log.

Good to know

  • With a Timer running, Mark a time shades the minutes until it ends.
  • Time in words writes the time under the clock, such as quarter past ten.
  • LCD look shows the digital time like an alarm clock. In dark theme it's LED look, and it glows.
  • A set time is saved with the board. The Set badge stays until you uncheck Set the time.
  • For a small clock on every page, set Settings › Advanced › Clock in the corner to On.
{{icon:calendar}}

Calendar

This month at a glance, with today highlighted.

Try it

  1. Open Calendar. Today is highlighted.
  2. Tap ‹ or › for another month.
  3. Tap Today to come back.

Ideas for class

  • K–2Point to today in morning meeting, then count the days to Friday.
  • 2–4Count the days to the field trip.
  • 6–8Plan due dates. Choose Monday in Calendar ⌄ so each row matches the school week.

Good to know

  • It shows dates. You can’t add events.
  • It moves to the new day by itself at midnight.

Words and pictures Notes, Picture, Video Player

{{icon:notes}}

Notes

A sticky note, a chalkboard, or a whiteboard for your words.

Try it

  1. Open Notes and type.
  2. The words grow or shrink to fill the note.
  3. Tap Notes ⌄ for Sticky note, Chalkboard, or Whiteboard, and a color.

Ideas for class

  • K–2The morning message on Chalkboard. Its chalk letters use a print l and a flagged 1, the way children learn them.
  • 3–5One note per center, each in its own color.
  • 6–8The bell-ringer on Whiteboard, tonight’s homework on a pink note beside it.

Good to know

  • Type a web address and it becomes a link.
  • Move it by the title bar or with two fingers, since you type in it.
{{icon:image}}

Picture

Show any picture you open, paste, or drop.

Three ways to add a picture

  • OpenOpen Picture, then tap Open…
  • PasteCopy a picture, then press Ctrl+V on the board. On a touch screen, tap [W], then Paste.
  • DropDrag a picture file onto the board.

Ideas for class

  • K–2A photo for “I notice, I wonder”, with a note of sentence starters beside it.
  • 3–5Paste a labeled diagram from your slides, then choose Fill the board.
  • 6–8Drop a map or a primary source beside a Timer.

Good to know

  • PNG, JPEG, GIF, WebP, AVIF, and SVG. A GIF keeps moving.
  • An iPhone photo in HEIC won’t open. Save it as a JPEG first.
{{icon:youtube-player}}

Video Player

A YouTube, Google Drive, or Vimeo video in a window.

Try it

  1. Copy a video’s link.
  2. Press Ctrl+V on the board, or tap [W], then Paste.
  3. A Video Player opens, ready to play.

Ideas for class

  • K–2Keep a brain-break video on your morning page. Its History holds your last 5.
  • 3–5A link with a start time plays from that moment.
  • 6–8Play a lesson you recorded and saved in Google Drive.

Good to know

  • It needs the Internet. Your school’s YouTube settings still apply.
  • 21Board saves the link, never the video.

Math and chance Calculator, Dice, Name Picker

{{icon:calculator}}

Calculator

Big, clear math, one step at a time.

Try it

  1. Tap the keys or type. The answer shows as you go.
  2. Tap Next step to work through it one step at a time.
  3. Turn on History in Calculator ⌄.

Ideas for class

  • K–2Choral count. Type 10 + 10, then press = again and again. Grade 2 can try 5 + 5.
  • 3–6Order of operations. Type 2 + 3 × 4, then Next step. Each step lights its rule.
  • 4–5Remainders. Pick With remainders in Calculator ⌄, then type 17 ÷ 5.
  • 6–7Percent. Turn on Advanced keys in Calculator ⌄, then type 15 % 80. It shows 15% of 80 = 12.

Good to know

  • Hide the answer lets the class predict first.

The Calculator’s own manual

{{icon:dice}}

Dice

Roll one to six dice and see the total.

Try it

  1. Tap Roll, or tap the dice.
  2. They tumble blank, then land one by one.
  3. Tap Dice ⌄ for 1 to 6 dice.

Ideas for class

  • K–2Turn off Show total. Students add, then turn it on to check.
  • 2–3Roll 3 dice. Who can make the biggest three-digit number?
  • 4–5Roll 6 dice. Who can make the biggest six-digit number?
  • 6–7Predict the most common total. Then roll 2 dice 30 times and tally.

Good to know

  • The dice stay blank while they tumble, so no one sees a number early.
{{icon:name-picker}}

Name Picker

Picks one to five students at random, with a little show.

Try it

  1. Type or paste your class list, one name per line, and tap Done.
  2. Tap SPIN, or Pick a name.
  3. Tap Name Picker ⌄ for Big name, Spinning wheel, Spotlight, or Lotto.

Ideas for class

  • K–2Pick the line leader with the Spinning wheel.
  • AnyCold-call fairly. No repeats is on, so everyone gets a turn before anyone goes twice.
  • 3–5Make groups. Set Winners each time to 4.

Good to know

  • Pick up to five names at once. Choose how many under Winners each time in Name Picker ⌄.
  • Names are only stored on your computer and your school Google Drive.
  • A Blank template never keeps the names, so it’s safe to share.

Routines Traffic Light, First/Then Board, Zones Check-In, Weather

{{icon:traffic-light}}

Traffic Light

A red, yellow, and green light you tap to change.

Try it

  1. Tap a light to turn it on. The others go dark.
  2. Tap it again to turn it off.
  3. Drag the window wide to turn it sideways.

Ideas for class

  • K–2Voice levels. Green for partner talk, yellow for a whisper, red for silent.
  • 3–5Green: work with a partner. Yellow: ask three before me. Red: on your own.
  • AnySet a Timer for work time and turn on Follow timers. Yellow means wrap up.

Good to know

  • Turn on Follow timers in Traffic Light ⌄. Green while a Timer runs, yellow near the end, and red at zero.
  • A tap still works. Your color stays until the Timer’s color changes.
  • It doesn’t listen to the room.
{{icon:first-then-board}}

First/Then Board

What comes first and what comes next, in pictures.

Try it

  1. Tap the FIRST card and pick a picture, or search.
  2. Tap the THEN card and pick what comes next.
  3. When the first task is done, tap ⭐ and choose First done.

Ideas for class

  • K–2Math ➔ Snack time, from Presets in the menu. When math is done, the Snack time card glows.
  • AnyDeep breaths ➔ a favorite activity, with the 💜 Calm theme.
  • K–5Clean up ➔ Line up before lunch.

Good to know

  • 62 picture cards in 7 groups.
  • Turn off Sound for a quiet room, and Animation to calm the motion.
{{icon:zones-check-in}}

Zones Check-In

Students tap the zone that matches how they feel.

Try it

  1. Students tap their zone’s card as they come in.
  2. Tap − on a card to take back a wrong tap.
  3. Tap Ideas for ways to calm down or wake up, with a breathing circle.
  4. Set your class size in Zones Check-In ⌄ › Class size.

Ideas for class

  • K–2Morning arrival. Each student taps a card before sitting down.
  • 3–5Check in again after recess. Start over is in the menu.
  • 6–8Turn off Show counts, so no one sees how many chose each zone.
  • AnyOpen Ideas for the zone most students chose, and breathe with the circle together.

Good to know

  • Fill the board and the zones line up in one row, in every student’s reach.
  • Counts clear each new day. Turn off Clear each day in the menu to keep them.
  • Counts are only stored on your computer and your school Google Drive.
  • A Blank template never keeps the counts.
{{icon:weather}}

Weather

The weather outside, and what to wear.

Try it

  1. Open Weather. It shows Wheeling.
  2. Make the window bigger to see more.
  3. Tap a part, such as the week, to see all of it. Tap ‹ Back to return.
  4. Tap Weather ⌄ › Location… for another town.

Ideas for class

  • K–2Weather helper. Turn on Simple words in Weather ⌄. The helper reads the sentence and the clothes.
  • K–2When a warning shows, tap it. Talk about how a forecast keeps us safe.
  • 3–5Open a second Weather for a town far away. How is its week different?
  • 6Choose °C and km/h on a cold day. Find the high and the low on a number line.
  • 6–8The polar vortex. In Weather ⌄ › On a date…, look up Jan 30, 2019. Compare it with today.

Good to know

  • It never asks where you are. It sends only a town, nothing about you or your class.
  • Offline, it shows the last forecast it saved.
  • Warnings always show. Hide watches in Weather ⌄.
  • Turn on No motion in Weather ⌄ for a still sky.

What to wear

What to wear goes by what the temperature feels like from 8 AM to 3:30 PM, or to 12 PM on a half day. The coldest hour picks the row. A day is warm only when its coldest hour is 60°F or more. Rain and snow add to any row.

District apps 21Sketch, 21Timer, 21DocCam

{{icon:21sketch}}

21Sketch

The district’s whiteboard app, right on your board.

Try it

  1. Open 21Sketch and draw.
  2. The same tools sit on both sides, so you can reach them from either side of the panel.
  3. Tap the grid button until you see graph paper, handwriting lines, or a music staff.

Ideas for class

  • K–2Handwriting lines. Model a letter, then invite a student to trace it.
  • 3–5Sketch a shape with the Smart Pen and it snaps clean.
  • MusicThe five-line staff, for notes the class can sing.

Good to know

  • It needs the Internet.
  • 21Sketch saves its drawing on this device. To keep a copy, use Save Project in 21Sketch.
{{icon:21timer}}

21Timer

The district’s classroom timer, with big numbers and chimes.

Try it

  1. Choose a time in Duration (min).
  2. Tap ▶ to start.
  3. Tap the numbers to pause.

Ideas for class

  • 3–5Twenty minutes of reading. Set Warn at (min) to 2 and turn on its 🔊 for a chime before the end.
  • 6–8Long labs and work blocks, up to 180 minutes.

Good to know

  • It needs the Internet. When the Wi-Fi is down, use 21Board’s own Timer.
{{icon:21doccam}}

21DocCam

The district’s document camera, live on your board.

Try it

  1. Open 21DocCam and allow the camera.
  2. Put the page under the camera. Rotate or mirror it until it reads right.
  3. Freeze Frame holds the picture still.

Ideas for class

  • K–2Read-aloud. Every seat sees the page.
  • 3–5Freeze a student’s work and circle the strategy with the Pen Tool.
  • 6–8Zoom in on a leaf or a ruler’s marks.

Good to know

  • It needs the Internet and a camera.
  • To keep a snapshot, tap Save Options › Copy to Clipboard, then Paste in the Widget Picker.

Make it yours

Arrange windows the way your room works, give each subject its own page, and pick the look your students read best.

Move, resize, fill

  • Tap a window to select it. Drag to move it, and pull a corner to resize.
  • Double-tap the title bar to fill the board.
  • Tap the name ⌄ for the window menu.
  • Close keeps what’s inside. Open the widget again and it’s all there.

A page for each subject

  • Tap ‹ Page › at the end of the Widget Picker.
  • On the last page, tap + to add one.
  • Tap Page, then Rename this page…
  • Try Morning, Math, and Science, or one page per class period.

Letters made for young readers

Settings › Appearance › Text style changes the letters on the board and in most widgets.

  • Early readers has an a and a g shaped the way children write them.
  • Easier to read uses Atkinson Hyperlegible, made for people with low vision. I, l, and 1 look different.

Colors and photos

Settings › Appearance sets how your board looks.

  • Background gives each page its own color. Choose one of ten, or tap Custom… for any color.
  • Photo… puts a picture behind the board. Fade softens it so the windows stand out.
  • Accent color tints the title bars and buttons. Pick one of six, or Custom… for your school colors.
  • Set Theme to Dark for lights-off moments.
  • Back row can’t read it? Settings › Advanced › Size of windows and menus, then tap +. It sets the page you’re on.

Lock the board

Tap [W], then [L] in its bottom row, and choose.

  • Lock everything for a video or a test. Nothing responds to a tap.
  • Widgets still work lets students start a Timer, roll the Dice, and type in Notes. Windows stay put.
  • To unlock, hold [L] in a top corner for 2 seconds.
  • It’s not a password. A tap on [L] says “Hold to unlock.”

Stay awake

Keeps the screen from dimming during a long reading block.

  • Settings › Classroom › Stay awake.
  • Turn it off when you step away, since a screen that never sleeps never locks.

Keep it safe

21Board saves as you work, keeps earlier versions, and can carry your board from your laptop to the smartboard.

Go back in time

A curious student closed everything? 21Board quietly saves earlier versions of your board.

  1. Open Settings and tap Board history…
  2. Tap a time to see how the board looked.
  3. Tap Go back. Changed your mind? Tap Undo.

An empty board also says “Windows gone?” Tap Go back to an earlier version to open Board history.

Back up now and then

Settings › Advanced › Back up now saves every page in one .21board file. Keep it in your school Google Drive or on a USB drive.

Import board in the Widget Picker opens one on any device. To share with your team, tap Export board in the Widget Picker and choose a Blank template. It keeps the layout without notes or names.

Laptop to smartboard

Board Sync keeps one board on all your devices, through a folder in your school Google Drive.

  1. Settings › Board Sync › Set up…
  2. Choose folder… In My Drive, make a folder named 21Board.
  3. Name this device, such as Laptop.
  4. On the smartboard, do the same and pick the same folder.

Works in Chrome and Edge on computers and Chromebooks. Not on an iPad, or on a shared panel no one signs in to.

One thing to remember

Your board lives on the device. Clearing the browser’s data, or a reset Chromebook, erases it there. Back up, or turn on Board Sync, before that happens.

Your work stays yours

Your boards, notes, name lists, and pictures are stored only on this device. 21Board has no servers and no accounts, so there’s nowhere else for them to go. Turn on Board Sync and Chrome also keeps a copy in a folder you choose, in your school Google Drive. When a video plays, YouTube, Google Drive, or Vimeo see what they would on their own sites. Weather sends only a town, nothing about you or your class.

Build your own widget

Have an idea for your class? Describe it in plain words, and an AI tool such as Gemini writes it. No coding needed.

  1. Get the prompt

    Open the 21Board AI prompt and copy the full prompt. Paste it into a new chat. In Gemini, use Canvas.

  2. Describe it

    Who uses it, the one main button, and what it should remember. Ask for changes in plain words.

  3. Try it on your board

    Save the file. In Settings › Advanced, turn on Developer mode, then tap Upload file…

Questions

Do I need to press Save?

No. Every change is saved on this device as you work. There’s nothing to sign in to.

I closed a window by mistake. Is it gone?

No. Tap [W] and open the same widget. It comes back with everything in it.

A student closed every window. Now what?

The empty board says “Windows gone?” Tap Go back to an earlier version. If someone used Close all, tap Undo on its message.

The windows won’t move.

If [L] shows in a top corner, the board is locked. Hold it for 2 seconds until the ring fills. Notes and other windows you type in move by the title bar.

Everything looks small on the big panel.

Open Settings › Advanced › Size of windows and menus and tap +. It sets the page you’re on. Full screen in the Widget Picker gives the board the whole screen.

Will a Timer ring if I switch pages?

No. Only the page you’re on runs. The Timer shows the right time when you come back.

Does it work without Wi-Fi?

Yes, once it has opened online on that device. Video Player, 21Sketch, 21Timer, and 21DocCam need the Internet. Weather shows the last forecast it saved. Board Sync catches up once you’re back online.

Why are there gray bands beside my board?

The board is widescreen, so it looks the same on every screen. To use the whole window, tap Fill the window on the tip, or choose Fill in Settings › Appearance › Board shape.

Can my co-teacher see my board?

Only if you share your 21Board folder with them in Google Drive as an Editor. Then you share one board through Board Sync.

Which version do I have?

Open Settings. The About row shows it, such as Version {{version}}. Read it to IT when you ask for help.
